Questions

Do I need to know any Latvian?

No. The course starts from zero and explains everything in English.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Langas is available for Android on Google Play.

What do I get with the full version?

Every lesson in every unit, including units we add later. You pay once through Google Play.

I changed phones. Do I lose my progress?

No. Your progress and purchase are saved to your account. Sign in on the new phone, and tap "Restore purchase" if needed.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Google Play, so refunds follow Google Play's refund rules. You can request one from your Google Play order history.
