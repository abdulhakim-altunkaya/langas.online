LANGAS: LEARN LATVIAN

You can ask us to delete your Langas account and the data linked to it at any time. Fill in the form below, or email drysoftware1@gmail.com with the subject "Langas – delete my account".

What gets deleted

  • Your account, display name and profile photo
  • Your progress, scores, streaks, crowns and achievements
  • Your friends list, congratulations and messages
  • Sign-in sessions on our server

What we keep

Records of purchases (order number and date) are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires. They are no longer linked to a profile. Payments themselves are handled by Google Play.

We complete deletions within 30 days and confirm by email. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete your account.
